Delete your 1Million Secrets account

1Million Secrets by 995 Studio

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open 1Million Secrets and sign in.
  2. Tap Account, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by signing in again (with your password, Google or Apple), then tap Permanently delete.

Deletion starts right away: you are signed out on all devices and stop receiving messages.

Option 2: By email

If you can't use the app, email support@995studio.com with the subject "Delete my 1Million Secrets account", from the email address you used to sign in. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We will not ask for an identity document. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Neither does signing out, hiding messages or turning off receiving.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, email address, name and avatar.
  • Your sent and received history, account notices and payment history view.
  • Messages you sent that are still waiting for delivery (withdrawn and erased).
  • The text of messages you sent that were already delivered. Recipients see "This message was removed" instead.
  • Your device and notification registrations.
  • Your unused credits and Remove ads purchase. These cannot be restored or moved to another account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase, refund and credit ledger records: up to 7 years, for tax, accounting, refund and fraud obligations.
  • Report and ban records linked to the account: as long as needed to keep bans effective, prevent ban evasion and handle legal claims.
  • Request-processing records: 30 days. Server logs (no message text or names): about 30 days.
  • Backups, where they exist: until they expire in their normal cycle.

Messages other people sent to you stay in their own Sent history until they delete their accounts. Copies someone made outside the app, such as screenshots or shared text, are beyond our control.

Refunds

Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so request refunds from Apple or Google Play. There are no subscriptions to cancel.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can hide individual messages in the app, change your name or avatar, and turn off receiving or notifications in Account → Settings. To ask us to delete specific data, email support@995studio.com with the subject "Privacy request". See the Privacy Policy for details.
