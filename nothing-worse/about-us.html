Small Ideas, Made With Care

995 Studio is an independent creative brand focused on making simple, thoughtful apps with warmth, personality, and a little unexpected humor.

We believe an app does not need to demand hours of attention to make a meaningful difference. Sometimes, one amusing sentence and a brief moment of escape are enough to make the day feel lighter.

That belief inspired Nothing Worse.

About Nothing Worse

Nothing Worse is a playful daily humor app built around your imaginary parallel life.

Each day, another version of you encounters one weird, harmless little issue. After discovering what happened, you choose how to respond:

WhateverrWhateverr means you are ready to welcome the same silly challenge and enjoy whatever the day brings.
Nope! Not TodayNope! Not Today means your parallel self can keep the trouble while you wish for a peaceful, lucky day.

Nothing Worse does not predict the future or tell you how your day will unfold. It is simply a tiny daily ritual designed to offer a laugh, a playful choice, and a lighter way to begin the day.

Designed With Purpose

We created Nothing Worse to feel different from busy feeds and complicated wellness apps.

There are no lessons to complete and no pressure to spend more time inside the app. Open it, read your daily message, make your choice, enjoy the moment, and continue with your day.

Every detail—from the hand-drawn artwork and paper-inspired design to the strange little messages and expressive stickers—was created to make the experience feel warm, human, and unmistakably playful.

The humor remains intentionally lighthearted. Nothing Worse focuses on imaginary inconveniences and ridiculous everyday bad luck, never serious hardship or harmful situations.

Simple, Private, and Respectful

Nothing Worse is designed with simplicity and privacy in mind. Its core daily experience works on your device, helping the app remain quick, calm, and easy to enjoy.

We respect your attention as much as your privacy. Our goal is to create something you will be happy to visit each day, even if only for a few seconds.

Thank You

Nothing Worse may be about an imaginary parallel life, but the smiles it creates in this one are real.

Thank you for welcoming our strange little idea into your day. We hope it gives you a laugh, makes you feel a little luckier, and reminds you that whatever happens, it could always be weirder.
