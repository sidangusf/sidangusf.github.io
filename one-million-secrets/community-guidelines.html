Community Guidelines

1Million Secrets · Version 1.0 · Effective date: November 10, 2026

Every message on 1Million Secrets lands in one real person's inbox. They didn't choose you, they can't reply, and they can't see who you are. That makes kindness your responsibility. These guidelines are part of our Terms of Service.

Good things to send

  • Something you've never said out loud: a worry, a hope, a regret, a small win.
  • Encouragement for whoever reads it.
  • A thought, a memory, an observation about your day.

Sadness, anger and hard feelings are welcome. Expressing pain is not the same as hurting someone.

Never send

  • Harassment or bullying: insults, intimidation or targeting anyone.
  • Hate or discrimination: attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or similar traits.
  • Threats or violence: threatening, promoting or glorifying harm to anyone.
  • Sexual content: sexually explicit material, sexual solicitation or non-consensual intimate content.
  • Anything involving minors: any sexual content involving minors, grooming, or trying to contact or target children. We report child sexual exploitation to the authorities where required.
  • Encouraging suicide or self-harm, or instructions for hurting yourself or others.
  • Spam, scams or deception: asking for money, fake offers, phishing, malware or misleading links.
  • Personal information: your own or anyone else's name, phone number, email, address, social media handle, workplace, photos, or anything that reveals who someone is.
  • Impersonation, defamation or illegal content, or content that infringes someone's copyright or privacy.

Don't try to get around the filter with misspellings, spaces, symbols or code words. The rule is about what you mean, not just the exact words.

Protect yourself

  • The person who receives your message can share it. Write only what you're comfortable seeing in public, without anything that identifies you.
  • Never send money, meet up, or share contact details because of a message from a stranger.
  • If a message upsets you, you can hide it, block its sender, or report it.

How reporting works

Open a received message and tap Report. Choose the reason that fits best and, if you like, add a note of up to 250 characters. The sender never learns who reported them. Each message can be reported once.

Please report honestly. Coordinated or malicious false reports break these guidelines, and we void reports we find were invalid.

What happens when rules are broken

Reports counted against an accountResult
1–2Recorded, no ban yet
3Banned for 3 days
4Banned for 7 days
5 or moreBanned permanently

Counts last for the life of the account and don't reset after a ban ends. A banned account can't send, receive, watch rewarded ads or buy credits. We may also restrict or close an account immediately for serious violations such as threats or anything involving minors, without waiting for reports.

If you think a ban was a mistake, email support@995studio.com from your account's email address. See section 6 of the Terms for how reviews work.

1Million Secrets is not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services right away.
