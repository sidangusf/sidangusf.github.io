The Story Behind Nothing Worse

A tiny daily ritual for weird little issues

A tiny daily ritual for weird little issues, gentle laughs, and wonderfully uneventful days.

It Started With a Small Thought

Some days feel difficult before they have even begun. A strange mood, a small worry, or one inconvenient moment can make everything feel heavier than it really is.

Nothing Worse began with a simple idea: what if one tiny, ridiculous story could interrupt that feeling?

Not with serious advice. Not with forced positivity. Just a harmless laugh and a gentle reminder that today might still turn out perfectly fine.

Somewhere, There Is Another You

In the world of Nothing Worse, you have a parallel life.

Every day, that other version of you encounters one weird, harmless little issue. Nothing frightening or truly terrible—just the kind of silly trouble that makes you pause and think, “Seriously?”

Maybe the universe misplaced the instructions. Maybe good luck took a suspiciously long lunch break. Whatever happened, your parallel self has already found today’s tiny inconvenience.

Now the choice belongs to you.

One Message, Two Ways to Begin

After reading your daily message, you can respond in one of two playful ways.

WhateverrChoose Whateverr when you are ready to accept the challenge and enjoy that same strange little issue in your own day. It is your way of saying, “Fine, bring it over. You cannot ruin my mood.”
Nope! Not TodayChoose Nope! Not Today when your parallel self can keep that issue. You are leaving the nonsense behind and wishing yourself a peaceful, lucky, trouble-free day.

There is no wrong choice. Nothing Worse is not predicting your future or deciding what will happen. It simply gives you a funny little moment to react to in your own way.

Why We Created It

Many apps ask for more of your attention. Nothing Worse was created to give something back: a quick laugh, a lighter thought, and a reason to begin the day with a little personality.

You do not need to complete a lesson, maintain a perfect streak, or become a different person. Open the app, discover today’s message, make your choice, and continue with your day.

Sometimes feeling better does not require a big solution. Sometimes it begins with one ridiculous sentence.

Humor With a Gentle Heart

The humor in Nothing Worse is intentionally weird, playful, and harmless.

The daily messages avoid truly painful or frightening situations. They focus on imaginary bad luck, tiny inconveniences, awkward universe energy, and the wonderfully unnecessary problems a parallel life might experience.

The goal is never to make light of real hardship. It is to turn small everyday frustration into something you can smile at.

A Tiny Daily Ritual

Nothing Worse is designed as a calm, single-purpose experience.

Each day brings one new message, one expressive reaction, and one simple choice. There is no endless feed to chase and no pressure to stay longer than you want.

It is a small daily ritual made for the moment before work, during a quiet break, or whenever the day needs a harmless reset.

Read it. React to it. Laugh at it. Then let the day continue.

Made to Feel Different

Every part of Nothing Worse was created to feel warm, handmade, and slightly unexpected.

The playful illustrations, meme-inspired cards, humorous stickers, and paper-like visual style give each daily message its own personality. The experience is intentionally simple, but never empty.

Behind that simplicity is a large collection of original messages and reaction artwork, carefully created so each new day can feel fresh while remaining unmistakably Nothing Worse.

Tomorrow, the Story Continues

Today’s tiny issue belongs to today. Tomorrow, your parallel life will return with something new.

Maybe you will welcome it with Whateverr.

Maybe you will send it away with Nope! Not Today.

Either way, we hope you leave with a smile, feel a little luckier in this life, and remember one comforting thought:

Whatever today brings, it could always be weirder.
