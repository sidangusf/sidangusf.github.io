Phone to desktop

Native on your phone.
Work on your computer.

Designed for iOS and Android, with a PromptVoy VS Code extension connecting your desktop sessions. The agent and its tools run on your computer, which needs to stay awake, connected, and signed in.

  1. 1

    Connect your desktop

    Install the companion extension, sign in, and approve your phone’s access on the computer.

  2. 2

    Choose a supported session

    Open a conversation exposed by the connected agent host. Provider compatibility is being validated.

  3. 3

    Continue from your phone

    Send prompts, follow the response, and review the agent’s work.
