How it works

One message, one person, once

There's no feed to scroll and no conversation to keep up. Just a thought, sent to one person you'll never meet.

1

Write

Up to 2,000 characters. Words that look like contact details or harmful content are highlighted, and you remove them before sending.

2

Send to a stranger

Your message goes to exactly one randomly selected person. Your Sent list shows it as Sent while it travels, then Received once it lands.

3

Receive one back

Turn on Receive automatically and a message from someone else can find you. Notifications never show the message text.

Safety first

Anonymous to each other, not a free-for-all

Filtered before sending

Emails, phone numbers, links, social handles and harmful words are caught on your phone and again on our server.

Report, block, hide

Report a message for a clear reason, block its sender without learning who they are, or hide it from your inbox.

Reports have consequences

Accounts that collect reports are banned automatically: 3 days, then 7 days, then permanently.

Honest about anonymity. Other users never see your name, email or account. 1Million Secrets itself does keep records of who sent what, so we can run the service, handle reports and meet legal duties. The person who receives your message can also copy or share it. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
